# Support

Gridnote is an early personal-Mac preview maintained on a best-effort basis.

## Getting Help

- Read the [README](README.md) and [known limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) first.
- Search existing [issues](https://github.com/ZOB2077/Gridnote/issues).
- Open a bug report with the app version, macOS version, hardware architecture, and reproducible steps.

Do not upload private books, real business data, credentials, security-scoped bookmark files, or personal information. Use a minimal synthetic fixture when demonstrating a problem.

## Boundaries

Community support does not cover DRM removal, monitoring bypass, full spreadsheet compatibility, unsupported formats, or commercial redistribution. Security issues must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Commercial licensing inquiries should be directed to [ZOB2077](https://github.com/ZOB2077).
